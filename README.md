# LoKAIT

**Local Knowledge AI Teacher**  
A rugged, solar-powered, offline AI education device for children in underserved and conflict-affected regions.

## Goals
- Deliver STEM and life skills to offline communities
- Use low-power AI (1–4B parameter models)
- Survive extreme weather, shock, and dust
- Open hardware for global adaptation

## Get Involved
- Hardware, Software/AI and Pedagogy contributors welcome
- Looking for hardware designers, STEM pedagogy experts and AI optimizers
